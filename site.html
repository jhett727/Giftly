What you get

Fair draws

Winners are picked at random. Bonus roles raise your odds in proportion to their entries, nothing more.

Checked at the end

If a winner left the server or lost the required role, Giftly skips them and draws again.

Entry rules

Require a role, block a role, or set a minimum account age or time in the server.

Reroll without drama

Swap out a winner who never replied, or add more winners to a finished giveaway.

Your server, your look

Pick the color, button, footer and winner messages from a settings panel inside Discord.

Keeps running

Giveaways survive restarts and end on time, even if nobody is around.

Commands

/giveaway startCreate a giveaway with a prize, duration, winners and entry rules. /giveaway endFinish one early and draw the winners. /giveaway rerollDraw again, or replace a specific winner. /giveaway editChange the prize, winner count or end time. /giveaway cancelStop a giveaway without a draw. /giveaway listSee what's running in your server. /giveaway settingsOpen the settings panel.

Set up in a minute

  1. Tap Add to Discord and choose your server.
  2. Run /giveaway settings to set your color and roles.
  3. Run /giveaway start and fill in the prize and duration.
