What Giftly does

Giftly is a Discord bot. It posts giveaways in a server, collects entries when people press a button, and picks winners when the time runs out. It isn't made by Discord, and Discord doesn't endorse it.

Who can use it

You need a Discord account, and you need to be old enough to use Discord where you live (13 at the very least). To add Giftly to a server you need the Manage Server permission there. You also have to follow Discord's own Terms of Service and Community Guidelines while you use Giftly. If those ever clash with these terms, Discord's rules win.

Running giveaways

If you start a giveaway, you're the host, and the giveaway is your responsibility. In practice that means:

  • You provide the prize and get it to the winner. Giftly never holds, sends or guarantees prizes.
  • You set the rules for your giveaway and you follow the laws where you and your entrants live. Some places regulate giveaways and prize draws, especially anything involving cash or a cost to enter. Checking that is on you, not on me.
  • Giftly is for free giveaways. Don't use it for anything that makes people pay to enter, or for gambling of any kind.

Winners are picked at random from valid entries, and bonus roles give a person extra entries. If a winner turns out not to be eligible or never replies, the host decides what happens next, and /giveaway reroll is there for that. I can't promise any particular person will win, and I'm not a party to disputes between hosts and entrants.

What you can't do

Don't use Giftly for scams, fake giveaways or phishing, or to collect people's passwords or personal details. Don't try to rig a draw, enter with alternate accounts to get extra chances, or abuse a bug (tell me about it instead). Don't overload the bot or this website, reverse-engineer them to get around limits, or use Giftly to harass anyone. And don't use it to break the law or Discord's rules.

Your data

To work, Giftly stores a few things: Discord IDs for servers, channels, roles, hosts and people who enter; the details of each giveaway, like the prize, description and end time; how many entries a person has; and the settings a server picks in /giveaway settings. It doesn't read the messages in your server. If you win, it may send you a DM. I don't sell any of this. If you want a server's data or your own entries deleted, contact me (details at the bottom) and I'll take care of it.

Keeping Giftly running

Giftly runs on a low-cost hosting plan, so it can go offline, restart or run slowly, sometimes without warning. Giveaways are saved and should finish once it's back, but one might end late. I'll try to fix problems when I can, but I'm not promising any level of uptime. I may also change, add or remove features, and one day I might shut Giftly down. If that happens I'll post a notice on this site.
